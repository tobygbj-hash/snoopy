Wake phrases Toby can use

When wake phrase mode is on, start with one of these, then say what to search for. You can say both in one breath or in two steps.

  • "Hey Snoopy, find calm music for focus"
  • "Hi Snoopy" … then "search cozy cafes nearby"
  • "Okay Snoopy, look up how to plant tomatoes"
  • "Wake up Snoopy, search sunrise photos"
  • "Hello Snoopy" or "Attention Snoopy" also work
  • While the AI summary is reading, say "hey Snoopy, stop" or "stop"

Without a wake phrase

Turn off wake phrase mode to search immediately, for example:

  • "Search cozy cafes nearby"
  • "Look up how to plant tomatoes"
